Our mission

Reading should never depend on what you can afford.

We exist to put good books, reliable information and a quiet seat within reach of everyone in the city, whether you are in primary school or retired.

  • Open

    Anyone can walk in, read, and ask for help. Membership only adds borrowing.

  • Careful

    A collection chosen by readers, kept in good condition, and refreshed every season.

  • Connected

    The same catalogue, e-books and study rooms are available online, any hour.
