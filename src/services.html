Digital Services

The library, wherever you are.

Five services that make the library easier to use, with the timings and steps for each.

  • 01

    Online Catalogue

    Search every title in the library, check whether it is on the shelf, and place a hold before you travel in.

    Timings

    Available 24 hours a day, every day.

    How it works

    1. Open the catalogue and search by title, author or subject.
    2. Check the availability and shelf mark.
    3. Sign in with your card number to place a hold.
  • 02

    E-books & Audiobooks

    Borrow digital titles to read on a phone, tablet or e-reader. Loans return themselves, so there are never late fees.

    Timings

    Available 24 hours a day with an active membership.

    How it works

    1. Sign in with your library card number.
    2. Choose a title and tap Borrow.
    3. Download it for offline reading. It returns after 14 days.
  • 03

    Study-Room Booking

    Six quiet rooms for individuals and small groups, each with a table, power sockets and a whiteboard.

    Timings

    Daily from 9:00 to 19:00 in two-hour slots, closed on Sundays.

    How it works

    1. Choose a date and a free slot at the desk or online.
    2. Show your card on arrival.
    3. Release the room if you leave early so others can use it.
  • 04

    Printing & Scanning

    Black-and-white and colour printing, plus flatbed scanning straight to your email.

    Timings

    Monday to Saturday during opening hours. Black and white PKR 5 per page, colour PKR 25.

    How it works

    1. Email or bring your file on a USB drive.
    2. Pay at the desk and collect your pages.
    3. For scans, give us your email address and we send the file.
  • 05

    Free Wi-Fi

    Fast, open internet for everyone in the building, with no membership required.

    Timings

    Available throughout opening hours, sessions of up to three hours.

    How it works

    1. Connect to the ReadersHaven-Guest network.
    2. Open any web page and accept the terms.
    3. Reconnect after three hours if you wish to stay on.
