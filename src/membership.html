Late returns

What happens if a book is late.

  1. 01
    Grace period of two daysNo fee is charged until the third day after the due date.
  2. 02
    Daily late feeCharged per book per day, at the rate of your plan, up to a cap of PKR 300 per book.
  3. 03
    Borrowing pauses at PKR 500Once unpaid fees pass PKR 500 you cannot borrow until they are settled.
  4. 04
    Lost or damaged booksReplacement cost of the book applies; the late fee stops once it is reported.
How to join

Three steps, about ten minutes.

  1. Bring your documents

    CNIC or B-Form, plus a student ID for the Student plan.

  2. Fill in one form

    Available at the front desk. Family plans list each member.

  3. Pay and collect your card

    Pay the annual fee and borrow your first books the same day.
